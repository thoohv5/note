错相同位，加减相同倍

### 应用

$$
\frac{A}{1+q_{A}}
$$

$$

\frac{A}{1+q_{A}} \times q_{A}

$$

$$
\frac{A}{B} \times \frac{1+q_{B}}{1+q_{A}}
$$

$$
\frac{A}{1+q_{A}} + \frac{B}{1+q_{B}}
$$

$$
A \times B
$$
