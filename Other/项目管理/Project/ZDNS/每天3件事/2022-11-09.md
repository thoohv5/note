## 2022-11-09

### 广东移动解决DHCPD多进程

1. 替换DHCPD
2. 修改 ipam_manager.rb 删除一下内容 

```bash
DDI::ZShell.rm_rf("/var/run/dhcpd.pid")
DDI::ZShell.rm_rf("/var/run/dhcpd6.pid")
```

1. route_switch

```bash
execute_command("rm -rf /var/run/dhcpd.pid >>/dev/null 2>&1")
execute_command("rm -rf /var/run/dhcpd6.pid >>/dev/null 2>&1")
```

1. 重启ipam进程

1. 改代码前，v4/v6删除PID重启DHCPD
2. 改代码后，kill DHCP V4/V6 route_swtich 拉起业务是否正常
